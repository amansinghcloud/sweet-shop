The Madhuraj Tradition

Founded in 1990 by Shri Ramesh Kumar, Madhuraj Sweet House began with a singular devotion: crafting authentic Indian mithai using 100% pure cow desi ghee, hand-churned fresh mawa, and whole unadulterated dry fruits.

From morning bati preparations to delicate silver-vark layered kaju katlis, every batch is prepared according to generational family recipes that honor the diverse culinary heritage of Rajasthan, Bengal, and Old Delhi.

Today, Madhuraj has served over half a million celebratory tables across India, upholding our foundational promise: "Shuddh Desi Ghee aur Parampara ki Meethi Yaadein".
